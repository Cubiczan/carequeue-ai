## Tips for Press Success

4. **Multimedia**: Include screenshots, demo video, diagrams
5. **Social Proof**: Mention any beta testers, notable contributors
6. **Follow-Up**: Send one follow-up 3-5 days later
7. **Embargo**: Offer embargoed copies for major outlets
8. **Metrics**: Track opens, responses, and coverage
