# Month-End Close SOP

**Cadence:** business day +5
**Owner:** Controller
**Approver:** CFO

## Day-by-day

| Day | Activity | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| BD-1 | Pre-close checklist sent | Controller |
| BD+1 | Cash & bank reconciliations | Sr. Accountant |
| BD+2 | AR/AP cut-off, billing freeze | AR/AP leads |
| BD+3 | Inventory & cost of sales | Ops Finance |
| BD+3 | Manual accruals & reclasses | Controller |
| BD+4 | Trial balance review, intercompany eliminations | Controller |
| BD+4 | Variance commentary draft | FP&A |
| BD+5 | CFO review & sign-off | CFO |
| BD+5 | Close package distributed | Controller |
| BD+6 | Closed-loop review with Claude → Notion decisions | CFO + AI |

## Definition of Done

- All BS accounts reconciled, signed
- Variance > [threshold] explained
- Close memo published to `03 Monthly Close/YYYY-MM …/`
- Audit note generated to `07 Audit Trail/`
- Material decisions logged in Notion
